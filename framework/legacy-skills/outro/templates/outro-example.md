## Outro

### Script (30-90 seconds)

**Close the Loop:**
> You came into this video thinking the skill was writing better prompts. Now you know — the prompt is the last thing that matters. What actually determines whether AI gives you great results is what you put in before you ever type a question. You have the full Context Ladder now. Five levels. Each one compounds on the last.

**Recap:**
> Quick recap. Level one was Conversation — just talking to AI and giving it context within the chat itself. Level two was Reference — feeding it documents, examples, and data to work from. Level three was Memory — giving AI persistent context that survives across sessions. Level four was Tools — connecting AI to real systems so it can take action, not just talk. And level five was System — engineering the entire context window so every interaction starts informed. And honestly, if you only take away one thing — level three, Memory, is where most people get the biggest jump. That's the one that turns AI from a search engine into an actual collaborator.

**Unexpected Surprise:**
> Before we wrap up — one more thing. There's a mistake I see almost everyone make when they start applying the Context Ladder. They try to jump straight to level five. They want the full system before they've even practiced level one. Don't do that. Start at level one. Get genuinely good at giving context in a single conversation. Then layer up. The levels are sequential for a reason — each one teaches you something the next one needs.

**Emotional Payoff:**
> Here's what I genuinely believe. If you practice these five levels — even just the first three — you will fundamentally change how you work with AI. Not in some vague, theoretical way. Practically. Tomorrow. Whether you're a developer building agents or you're like my dad, who built his own expense tracker at 65 with no coding background. AI is for everyone. The only variable is whether you start climbing the ladder.

**Last Dab:**
> The prompt is just the last 1%. The other 99% is context. And now you know how to use all of it.

**CTA:**
> If this channel's mission — making AI accessible to everyone, not just developers — sounds like something you want more of, subscribe. And tell me in the comments: which level of the Context Ladder are you currently at? I read every single comment, and it directly shapes what I make next.

**Forever Loop:**
> Now that you understand how to give AI the right context, the next thing that's going to break is getting AI to take action on its own. I made a video about building your first AI agent that picks up exactly where this one leaves off — I'll link it right here.

**Sign-Off:**
> Until next time — keep building.

### Templates Used
- Close the loop: "Close the Loop (Pain → Resolution)" template adapted — instead of the full causal chain format, used a direct close because the Listicle structure has a clean "before/after" arc. "You came into this video thinking X, now you know Y" traces the transformation.
- Recap: "Recap (Eight-Step Numbered Playbook)" template adapted for 5 levels — "Level one was [name]. [One-line summary]." Ordinal structure. Added meta-summary: "if you only take away one thing — level three." Compressed pace, one line per level.
- Unexpected surprise: "Unexpected Surprise / Bonus Tip" template — "Before we wrap up — one more thing." The bonus is a common mistake (jumping to level 5 prematurely) which is genuinely useful and didn't fit the body's per-level structure.
- Emotional payoff: "Emotional Payoff (Optimism Close)" template blended with "Authentic Investment" — "If you practice these five levels... you will fundamentally change how you work with AI" (optimism) + "Whether you're a developer building agents or you're like my dad" (authentic investment via dual example). Dad story anchors accessibility. "The only variable is whether you start" removes excuses.
- Last dab: From /outline — delivered as-is. Share test: PASS — "The prompt is just the last 1%" is a portable, quotable reframe. Loop test: PASS — for a short-form derivative, this line sets up the hook's opening about prompts being overrated. Emotion peak: PASS — the "and now you know" payoff is empowering.
- CTA: Adapted from "Native Embed CTA (Free Community)" template — replaced Wavy World community CTA with subscribe + comment. Mission statement wrap ("making AI accessible to everyone, not just developers") is the authentic framing. Comment invite is specific to this video: "which level of the Context Ladder are you currently at?" — this answer gives genuine content pipeline signal (are viewers at level 1? level 3? this shapes future video topics).
- Sign-off: "Until next time — keep building." — Calm, warm, action-oriented. "Keep building" echoes the channel's ethos (build real things with AI) and serves as an implicit call to action. This is the proposed HitL formula, not Kallaway's "peace."
