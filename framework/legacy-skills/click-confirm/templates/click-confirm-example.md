## Click Confirm

### Script (10-30 seconds)
> Today we're talking about context engineering — the real skill behind getting great results from AI. It goes way beyond prompting. I call this the Context Ladder. There are 5 levels. By the way, I'm Fahad — 15 years building production software, last two years building AI systems every single day. My 65-year-old non-technical dad built his own apps using exactly what I'm about to show you. By the end of this, you'll know how to structure your AI conversations at every level.

### Breakdown
- **Confirmation:** "Today we're talking about context engineering — the real skill behind getting great results from AI. It goes way beyond prompting." — Confirms the title's promise (prompting isn't the key skill) and exceeds it by naming the replacement skill (context engineering) and hinting at depth ("goes way beyond").
- **Framework Name:** "The Context Ladder" — coined in /structure, 2-4 words, hints at the benefit (climbing = progression), ownership language ("I call this")
- **Point Count:** 5 levels — matches the outline count exactly (Level 1: Conversation, Level 2: Reference, Level 3: Memory, Level 4: Tools, Level 5: System)
- **Credibility:** Level 2 (engineering experience — "15 years building production software, AI systems every day") + Level 4 (dad story — "65-year-old non-technical dad built his own apps") + Level 5 (common ground — "using exactly what I'm about to show you" implies if it works for him, it works for anyone). Placed after the plan (framework name + number) per Kallaway's plan-before-proof pattern.
- **Outcome Promise:** "you'll know how to structure your AI conversations at every level" — ties to the hook's pain point (people guess at prompts instead of using a system), verifiable (after watching, the viewer can check: do I know all 5 levels?), aligned with desired emotion (empowered)
- **BS Detector:** PASS — Credibility is specific and earned (15 years, 65 years old — real numbers, not vague). The dad story is compressed to one clause but retains the emotional punch. Plan-before-proof sequencing means the viewer already knows the roadmap (5 levels) before credentials are introduced — this reduces "who is this person?" friction because they're already invested in the plan. ~75 words, ~30 seconds at natural pace.

### Templates Used
- Click confirmation: "Direct context + confirm" template — "today we're talking about [SWAP: context engineering]." Adapted: compressed the template's 3-sentence structure into 2 sentences for pacing, since the hook already planted the pain point. "Goes way beyond prompting" is the exceed element.
- Framework naming: "Atomic messaging" variant — "I call this [SWAP: the Context Ladder]." Simple, one sentence, ownership language. Placed immediately after confirmation per plan-before-proof.
- Plan stated: "Plan Stated (number + category)" template — "There are [SWAP: 5] [SWAP: levels]." Direct, specific, no meta-structure needed (5 is a manageable number).
- Credibility drop: "By the way" aside template (adapted for HitL) — replaced Kallaway's social proof metrics (billion views, million followers) with Level 2 engineering credential + Level 4 dad story compressed to one clause. Positioned after the plan per Kallaway's actual pattern.
- Outcome promise: "If you get this, you'll..." template — "by the end of this, you'll know how to [SWAP: structure your AI conversations at every level]" — specific, verifiable, tied to the hook's pain point.

### Proof Level Notes
- **Currently available:** Level 2 (15 years engineering), Level 4 (dad story), Level 5 (common ground), Level 6 (production quality)

Level 4 fits because the video promises "for everyone" — the dad story proves it works for non-technical people. Level 5 is implicit in the dad story ("if it works for him, it works for anyone").
