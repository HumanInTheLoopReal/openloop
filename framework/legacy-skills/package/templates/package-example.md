## Package

- **Working Title:** Why Prompting Is the Least Important AI Skill
- **Title Direction:** Pain point
- **Title Template Used:** Pain-framed: "Why [SWAP: common thing everyone does] Is [SWAP: wrong / costing you / backwards]"
- **Desire Loop:** Triggers the pain of "I've been focused on the wrong thing" — the IVA has been watching prompt engineering videos and trying to write better prompts, but their results are still inconsistent. This title tells them the problem isn't their prompts, it's something else entirely. Opens a knowledge gap they need to close.

### Title-Thumbnail System
- **Title covers:** pain (your prompting focus is wrong) + solve (implies there IS something more important)
- **Thumbnail covers:** proof (visual showing the context ladder — the actual framework that replaces prompting)

### Thumbnail Concept
- **Elements (3):** Visually compelling graphic (context ladder with 5 labeled rungs) + Large text ("NOT Prompting") + Color science (copper/charcoal palette against blue AI niche)
- **Composition:** Symmetrical — ladder graphic centered, text above, warm copper glow behind
- **Color:** Dark charcoal (#2d2d2b) background with warm copper (#cc7d5e) accent on the ladder rungs and text highlight. This inverts the cold blue/neon green dominant in AI thumbnails. The warm copper reads as "human, approachable, different" in a sea of tech-cold designs.
- **Visual:** End state — the 5-level Context Ladder fully rendered, showing the complete framework the viewer will learn. Each rung labeled (Conversation → Reference → Memory → Tools → System). This serves as proof that there's a real, structured framework inside — not just a rant about prompting being bad.
- **Dark mode:** Verified — charcoal background with copper accents pops strongly against YouTube's dark gray (#1f1f1f). The warm copper creates natural contrast without relying on brightness.
- **Concept description:** A clean, centered ladder graphic with 5 copper-colored rungs on a charcoal background. Each rung is labeled with a context level. Above the ladder, bold text reads "NOT Prompting" in off-white (#f9f9f7). The overall feel is warm, structured, and authoritative — it says "there's a real framework here" without any face needed. At 1/16th iPhone size, the ladder shape and "NOT Prompting" text remain readable.

### Trust Score Assessment
- **IVA threshold:** 35-45 — This is a semi-active problem. The IVA isn't in crisis, but they've felt the frustration of inconsistent AI results. They've probably watched prompt engineering content and are open to a better answer. Not pure passive browsing (80+), not desperate problem (20). Mid-range willingness to click.
- **Current score:** ~65 — The title directly challenges something the IVA has been doing (prompting), creating a strong desire loop. The thumbnail provides visual proof of an organized framework. The color science pops. Weakness: no face means no emotional shortcut, and no existing channel authority (new creator). The framework visual partially compensates for the missing face by showing intellectual substance.
- **Weakest input:** Proof signal — As a new channel with no subscriber count, no face, and no established credibility, the proof vertex relies entirely on the framework visual and the title's confidence. Once the channel has some traction (views on previous videos, subscriber count visible), the trust score will climb. For now, the framework graphic does the heavy lifting.

### Peer Share Filter
- **Would IVA share?** Yes — "Why prompting is the least important AI skill" is a shareable contrarian claim. The IVA who learns this would share it with friends who are also trying to get better at AI: "Dude, watch this — turns out we've been focused on the wrong thing." It reframes their understanding, which is the highest-share-impulse category. The IVA shares videos that made them feel smart, not dumb — and learning "the real skill" when everyone else is stuck on prompting delivers exactly that feeling.
