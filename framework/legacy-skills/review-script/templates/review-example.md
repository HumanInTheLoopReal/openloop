### Specific Fixes Required

**Fix #1: Three "and then" transitions in Levels 2-3**

Location: Level 2, sub-point on file organization; Level 3, transition between template example and testing example.

Before (Level 2, sub-point transition):
> "First, you create a project file. Then you add your coding conventions."

After:
> "First, you create a project file. But here's what most people miss — your coding conventions aren't just style preferences. They're context the AI uses to make decisions."

Before (Level 3, between examples):
> "The template handles the structure. And then you test it with edge cases."

After:
> "The template handles the structure. But the structure is only as good as the edge cases you throw at it."

Before (Level 3, sub-point):
> "You write the rules once. Then the AI follows them every time."

After:
> "You write the rules once. The AI follows them every time — which means every mistake in your rules gets amplified too."

**Fix #2: Level 3 non-tech example needs development**

Location: Level 3, Application section, non-tech example.

Before:
> "For non-technical users, think of it like organizing a recipe collection — you're creating a system the AI can follow."

After:
> "Say you're a home cook who wants AI to help plan meals. Instead of asking 'what should I make for dinner?' every night, you create a structured prompt template: 'I have [these ingredients], I'm cooking for [this many people], allergies are [these], and I want something ready in [this many minutes].' You write that template once. Now every dinner question gets a useful answer — not because you got better at asking, but because you gave the AI the right context to work with."

**Fix #3: Level 3 missing contrast words**

Location: Level 3 body section, paragraphs 2-5.

Before (paragraph 3):
> "Structured prompts organize your context into clear sections. Each section tells the AI what role to play, what constraints to follow, and what output format you want."

After:
> "Structured prompts organize your context into clear sections. But it's not just about being neat — each section tells the AI what role to play, what constraints to follow, and what output format you want. Without that structure, the AI is guessing."

Add to paragraph 5:
> Before: "This gives you consistent results across different sessions."
> After: "This gives you consistent results across different sessions — instead of the lottery you get when you wing it every time."
